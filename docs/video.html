IntentRadar

Finds people on Reddit who are asking for what you sell — and publishes how often it is wrong.

Every number in this video can be recomputed by a stranger, offline, without an API key. That is the whole point.

03 — what we did not expect

The second model is a strict superset, not a contradiction

Nemotron accepted every post DeepSeek accepted, and 8 more. Nothing was reversed.

1

is 1woxhua — the one real buyer the stricter model missed. The entire reason recall goes 90% → 100%.

6

are confirmed false positives. The entire reason precision goes 100% → 62.5%.

1

is borderline — a human could not rule it, so it is excluded, not counted as a miss.

Not two models disagreeing about which posts are actionable — one model reaching further, and a visible price for the extra reach.

04 — the fine print

What this does not claim

  • n = 10. Ten real buyers. That is why every figure carries a 95% interval.
  • One product, five subreddits. Nothing here generalises on its own.
  • It did not run on Nebius. OpenRouter served it; the gateway reported provider "Nvidia". We fail that clause of the rules and say so.
  • 5 of the 42 judgements in the published run hit a 503 on the first attempt and were retried. Zero unresolved — but the first pass was not clean.
